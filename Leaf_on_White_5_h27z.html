Background Size

Full size is best for detailed leaves — Monstera fenestrations, fine variegation, thin stems. Faster shrinks each photo to 1600px first: quicker on a phone, but that downscaling can blur the little holes and edges the AI needs to see, so some background can survive inside them. You can flip White ↔ Black any time, even after photos are done — they re-set instantly, no re-processing.

How it works

  1. Add photos — one at a time or a whole batch.
  2. They process one by one, right here in your browser. The first photo takes longer: the full-precision AI model downloads once (it's the big, careful one — that's what lets it clean out the background showing through a Monstera's fenestrations), then your browser keeps it cached — after that, this page works even offline.
  3. Pick white or black, before or after processing — switching re-sets finished photos from the saved cutout, so there's no waiting through the AI again.
  4. Adjust if you need to — tap Adjust on a finished photo to keep just one leaf (tap the leaf you want; Whole plant brings everything back) or to drag and zoom it into a square, 4:5, or original-size frame until the crop looks right.
  5. Download each finished photo on its own, or grab the whole batch as one ZIP. Downloads always use your adjusted version.

One honest note: the tool keeps whatever is in the foreground — leaf, pot, moss pole, and the hand holding it. Walls, doors, and clutter behind the plant are what disappear. Keep your originals for the training dataset; these versions are the pretty ones for selling and sharing.

The AI runs 100% on your device. On first use, the AI library and model load from a CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net / esm.sh / staticimgly.com) — that first download needs internet, and Chrome works best. Nothing you process is uploaded anywhere.
