How it works

  1. Add photos — one at a time or a whole batch.
  2. They process one by one, right here in your browser. The first photo takes longer: a small AI model (~40 MB) downloads once, then your browser keeps it cached — after that, this page works even offline.
  3. Download each finished photo on its own, or grab the whole batch as one ZIP.

One honest note: the tool keeps whatever is in the foreground — leaf, pot, moss pole, and the hand holding it. Walls, doors, and clutter behind the plant are what disappear. Keep your originals for the training dataset; these white versions are the pretty ones for selling and sharing.

The AI runs 100% on your device. On first use, the AI library and model load from a CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net / esm.sh / staticimgly.com) — that first download needs internet, and Chrome works best. Nothing you process is uploaded anywhere.
